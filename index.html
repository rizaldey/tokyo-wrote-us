✳A SMALL GLIMPSE INTO RYU’S WORLD

The city was louder at night, but somehow it also felt more honest.

People walked a little faster, laughed a little louder, and said the things they probably wouldn’t in the morning.

And standing there, watching him smile at everyone around him, I realized that I wanted to be someone who could make him smile like that, too.

Maybe love doesn’t always arrive exactly when you planned it to.

01 / The story

Some meetings
stay with us.

Ryu had stopped expecting love to surprise him. His life in Tokyo was comfortable enough. A job, good friends, familiar routines, and the occasional reminder that maybe everyone else was moving forward a little faster than he was.

Then a cancelled flight changed the plans for one ordinary night. And Ryu met a stranger.

What began as coincidence became another meeting. Then another. Tokyo became Nagoya. Nagoya became Seoul.

Somewhere between missed trains, late-night conversations, photographs, and goodbyes that never quite felt final, Ryu began to wonder:

What if you meet the right person before the timing is ready?

02 / The cast

The people who crossed his path.

A few faces, a thousand little moments. Drawn in blue ink, like memories kept between the pages.

Ryu

Tokyo · The observer

Living in Tokyo, Ryu has learned to keep his world in order, even when his heart tells a different story. Beneath his wit and quiet longing lies a hope for something real. Then someone walks into his life, a stranger with an unforgettable smile and a presence that lingers long after goodbye, leaving Ryu to wonder if some encounters are written by chance, or something far more mysterious.

Meet the narrator ↗

Jihoon

Korea · The unanswered question

Some people enter your life when you least expect them and make ordinary moments feel extraordinary. For Ryu, Jihoon is an unexpected encounter that sparks curiosity, butterflies, and the possibility of something more. Sometimes, one person is all it takes to make you see love a little differently.

Kris

Manila to Tokyo · The catalyst

Ryu’s self-proclaimed best friend, whose visit to Tokyo sets an unexpected chain of events in motion. Always at the center of the laughter and little chaos, Kris brings more than just friendship into Ryu’s world. Sometimes, an unplanned visit and a change of plans are all it takes to set an entirely different story in motion.

CJ

Tokyo · The connector

The mastermind behind the group chats, meetups, and unexpected encounters. CJ always seems to have a plan and a reason to bring people together. But sometimes, the simplest invitation can lead to the most unexpected connections, setting a story in motion that no one could have planned.

Theo

Canada · The missed connection

A cancelled flight sets the story’s events in motion, connecting people who might otherwise never have crossed paths. Though his plans take an unexpected turn, Theo’s place in the story reminds us that sometimes, the most meaningful encounters begin when life refuses to follow the itinerary.

05 / Behind the pages

A note from the author.

Shougo Ohba is a Filipino-Japanese writer based in Tokyo. Through stories about timing, travel, and the people who leave a mark on us, he explores the in-between moments that can change a life, and the feelings that remain long after goodbye.

Some stories stay with us because they never quite happened.

Shougo Ohba

Thank you for stepping into Ryu’s world. This story is for anyone who has ever wondered whether a connection can be real, even when the timing is uncertain.
