01 / THE STORY

A story of its own.

Theo was supposed to land in Tokyo on February 12. His flight was canceled. So he took the next one. In a crowded café, a girl sat across from two cups. One had gone cold. She pushed the empty chair out for him.

Some decisions look small. A rebooked flight. A borrowed chair. A name not mentioned. Sometimes love makes you book flights. Other times, insecurity does. This is a story about how one small choice can change the lives around it.

BEHIND THE PAGES

A note from the author.

Shougo Ohba is a Filipino-Japanese writer based in Tokyo. Through stories about timing, travel, and the people who leave a mark on us, he explores the in-between moments that can change a life, and the feelings that remain long after goodbye.

This companion novel stands on its own. You can read it before or after the other book in the series.
